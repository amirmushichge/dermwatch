## Research inspiration

DermWatch was inspired by [OpenDerm](https://openderm.github.io/), an
open-source robotic 3D skin-imaging research project created by
[Marion Lepert](https://x.com/marionlepert).

- [Original OpenDerm announcement](https://x.com/marionlepert/status/2082512842742489258)
- [OpenDerm project](https://openderm.github.io/)
- [OpenDerm source code](https://github.com/MarionLepert/openderm)

OpenDerm is distributed under the MIT License. DermWatch does not redistribute
OpenDerm source code, CAD files, hardware designs, datasets, or model weights.
The attribution above acknowledges the research concept that motivated this
separate, software-only experiment.

DermWatch is an independent project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by
OpenDerm or Marion Lepert.

## Runtime model disclosure

DermWatch currently bundles no pretrained machine-learning model and no model
weights. It does not call an LLM, cloud inference API, or third-party medical
classifier. See [TECHNICAL_TRANSPARENCY.md](TECHNICAL_TRANSPARENCY.md).
