# DermWatch v0.2.4

DermWatch v0.2.4 makes the product's purpose clear from the first screen.

## What changed

- the main heading now explains that DermWatch tracks visible changes in moles;
- the supporting line describes the private photo-history workflow;
- the introductory copy explicitly states that DermWatch does not detect
  cancer or precancerous conditions;
- the wording stays readable on wide desktop and mobile layouts;
- the repository preview now uses a real public-domain demonstration photo
  instead of an abstract empty-state illustration.

## Medical limitation

DermWatch does not detect or rule out skin cancer, melanoma, dysplasia or any
precancerous condition. Its image measurements are experimental and have not
been clinically validated. New, changing, different, itching, bleeding or
otherwise concerning spots should be assessed by a qualified clinician.
