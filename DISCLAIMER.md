# Medical and research disclaimer

DermWatch does not detect or rule out skin cancer or melanoma. It is an
experimental visual monitoring tool, not a medical device, diagnostic system,
clinical decision-support system, cancer-risk score, or substitute for
professional medical care.

Its image-processing rules have not been validated in a clinical trial and do
not provide known diagnostic sensitivity, specificity, or accuracy for
melanoma or any other disease. A result such as "no pronounced visual features"
does not mean that a spot is safe or benign. A warning does not mean that a
spot is malignant.

Seek qualified medical assessment for a new or changing skin lesion, or for a
spot that bleeds, itches, hurts, ulcerates, or otherwise causes concern. Do not
delay professional care because of a DermWatch result.

Use of DermWatch is at the user's own risk.
